The game show where algorithms fight over who gets to call the shots.

Here's how it works

Two teams of little programs get dropped into a country they've never seen and have to find the shortest road trip through every city. There's no map. All they can do is try a route and ask how long it was, and every time they ask, it comes out of their budget.

On one side, {{team_c}}, where a small group calls the shots for everybody. On the other, {{team_d}}, where everybody calls their own. Both teams get the exact same cities, the exact same budget and the exact same dice rolls.

Shorter trips win the match. The losing team goes back, changes one thing, and comes back to challenge the champ. Then we do it again.
