evosoc

A continuous discovery, played as a game show. Two teams of genetic algorithms race to find the shortest round trip through the same hidden cities, with the same budget and the same luck. Team Centralizers lets a few decide for everyone; Team Decentralizers lets every member decide for itself. Each match tests one idea, and records what it taught us.
